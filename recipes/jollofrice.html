Jollof Rice Recipe

Description

Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, and a variety of spices. It is a flavorful and satisfying meal that is often served at special occasions and family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long grain rice
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs clear.
  2. In a large pot, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook until translucent.
  3. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute.
  4. Stir in the curry powder, thyme, and bay leaf. Cook for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the rinsed rice and stir to coat with the spices.
  6. Pour in the tomato sauce and bring to a boil.
  7. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes or until the rice is cooked and the liquid is absorbed.
  8. Remove from heat and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.
