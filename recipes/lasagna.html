Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish that consists of layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain any excess fat.
  3. Add the marinara sauce to the skillet and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  5. In a 9x13 inch baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom.
  6. Layer 4 lasagna noodles over the sauce, followed by a layer of ricotta cheese, a layer of meat sauce, and a layer of mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a layer of mozzarella cheese on top.
  7. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  8. Let the lasagna cool for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
