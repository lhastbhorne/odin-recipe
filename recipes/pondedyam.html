Pounded Yam Recipe

Description

Pounded Yam is a traditional West African dish made by mashing boiled yams with a mortar and pestle. It is often served with a variety of stews or sauces.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs yams, peeled and boiled
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the peeled yams in a large pot of water until they are soft and tender.
  2. Drain the yams and transfer them to a large mortar and pestle or a mixing bowl.
  3. Mash the yams until they are smooth and free of lumps.
  4. In a separate pan, heat the coconut milk over medium heat. Add the minced garlic, diced onion, curry powder, salt, and pepper. Cook for 5 minutes until fragrant.
  5. Pour the coconut milk mixture over the mashed yams and mix well until fully incorporated.
  6. Serve the pounded yam with your favorite stew or sauce.
